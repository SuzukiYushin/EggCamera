# Egg Camera Design Guidelines

When designing or modifying the Egg Camera app, always maintain the warm, family-oriented tone from familiar.co.jp. The brand emphasizes:

- **Visual Design**: Soft, pastel color palettes, clean layouts, family-friendly imagery
- **UX Principles**: Intuitive navigation, accessibility for parents and children, simple interactions
- **Tone**: Warm, supportive, celebratory of family moments
- **Functionality**: Focus on ease of capturing and sharing precious moments with loved ones

Ensure all changes enhance usability while preserving the brand's aesthetic and emotional connection.
